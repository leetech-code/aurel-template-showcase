Overview

AUREL is a reusable commercial e-commerce template designed for fashion brands, online retailers and businesses seeking a polished digital storefront.

It combines modern frontend design with a structured backend, persistent order management and payment integration.

* Responsive storefront design
* Product catalog and collections
* Shopping cart and wishlist
* Detailed product pages
* Complete checkout experience
* Paystack payment integration

* Persistent order management
* Administrative dashboard
* Product and inventory management
* Configurable store settings
* Exchange rate management
* Light and dark themes
